# Mixing basics
- Buses: Master, Music (-14 dB), SFX (-8), Ambience (-20), UI (-12), compressor on Master, music ducks 6 dB under big hits and voice.
- Loudness: music about -16 LUFS, SFX peaks under -3 dBFS, no clipping. Every SFX has 3 round-robin variants with +-6% pitch and +-1.5 dB volume.
- 3D sounds attenuate over 25 m; reverb zone per interior; footsteps change with the surface.
