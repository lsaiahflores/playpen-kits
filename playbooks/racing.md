# Racing feel
- Arcade handling: steering rate drops with speed, drift builds a mini-turbo after 1.2s, boost pads give 1.5s of +35% speed.
- Camera 5 m back and 2 m up, FOV widens with speed (+12 degrees), subtle shake at boost.
- Track: width 14-18 m, banked turns, a visible racing line, 3 laps, rubber-banding at most 8%.
- Sound: engine pitch follows speed, tire squeal on drift, distinct boost whoosh.
