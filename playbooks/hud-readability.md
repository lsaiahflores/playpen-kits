# HUD readability
- Safe margins 4% from every edge. Text at least 24 px at 1080p, 3:1 contrast, dark outline or backing plate.
- Max 5 persistent elements. Health is a bar plus a number; ammo a number plus an icon. Flash a changed value for 0.3s.
- Use one icon set and one font family everywhere. Menus must be controller-navigable with a visible focus state.
