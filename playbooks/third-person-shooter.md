# Third-person shooter feel
- Over-the-shoulder camera 2.2 m back, 0.6 m right, collision-pushed; aim zoom narrows FOV by 15 degrees. Camera never clips walls.
- Movement faces camera yaw when aiming; roll/dodge 0.5s with brief invulnerability; cover snap within 1 m.
- Weapons have recoil, spread that grows with fire rate, and a distinct sound per weapon.
- Enemies telegraph attacks 0.4s before they land. Mix 3 enemy roles (rusher, shooter, tank).
