# Music by genre
- Arena/action: minor key, 130-150 bpm, driving drums + bass ostinato, brass or synth stabs, a 4-bar motif varied each loop.
- Platformer/adventure: major or lydian, 100-124 bpm, bright lead (flute/marimba/strings), walking bass, call-and-response phrases.
- Racing: 140-160 bpm, synth bass, straight kick, lead with a repeating hook. Calm/explore: 70-90 bpm, pads + sparse piano.
- Always original: key, tempo, sections intro > loop > variation; stems base/rhythm/melody/intensity; never a copy or soundalike of a known theme.
