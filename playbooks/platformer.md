# Platformer feel
- Coyote time 0.12s, jump buffer 0.14s, variable jump height (release cuts upward speed by 50%), corner nudge 0.2 m.
- Landing squash + dust; every collectible has sparkle + a pitched pickup note in the music key.
- Camera leads the player's facing, never loses the ground below. Checkpoints every ~40 seconds of play.
- Platforms: gaps reachable at 85% of max jump; first 3 jumps are safe teaching jumps.
