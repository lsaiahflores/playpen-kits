# Making a toy-style character read at a glance
- Chunky proportions: head 35-40% of height, big hands/feet, thick limbs. A silhouette test: fill it black — role must still be clear.
- Three defining features only (visor, shoulder pads, backpack). Everything else stays plain.
- Two main colors + one accent. Enemies use a different hue family from the player. Glossy plastic: low roughness (0.25), a subtle rim light.
- Non-human creatures keep non-human posture and leg structure; never a recolored person.
