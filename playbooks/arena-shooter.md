# Arena shooter feel
- Fast: ground speed 9-11 m/s, jump ~1.4 m, air control 70%. Weapons swap in under 0.25s. Respawn within 3s at the farthest spawn from enemies.
- One readable arena: 3 height levels, 2-3 sight-line breakers, every corner has cover. Power weapons at contested spots on 30-60s timers.
- Hit feedback on every shot: crosshair tick, hit sound, 80ms hit-stop on kills, screen-edge damage direction.
- Bots: strafe while shooting, retreat to heal under 30% health, grab pickups, never aim perfectly (0.25s reaction, 12% miss cone).
- HUD: health/shield top-left, ammo bottom-right, crosshair centered, score top-center. 3 colors max.
