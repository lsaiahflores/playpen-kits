# Top-down feel
- Camera 55-65 degrees pitch, follows with 0.15s smoothing, never rotates unless the game needs it.
- Characters readable at 48 px: strong silhouette, outline or rim light, ground shadow blob.
- Move on an 8-way or free-aim scheme with 0.08s acceleration; attacks have 3 frames of anticipation.
- Level rooms are 8-14 tiles wide with one clear exit; enemies appear in groups of 2-4.
