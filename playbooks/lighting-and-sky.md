# Lighting and sky basics
- One key light (sun) at 35-55 degrees elevation with soft shadows, one fill from the sky color, a warm/cool contrast.
- Always a real sky (gradient or HDRI), fog matching the horizon color, tonemap + mild glow. Never the default grey environment.
- Ground uses a tiling texture with variation (not flat color); scatter 3+ small props per 10 m square.
